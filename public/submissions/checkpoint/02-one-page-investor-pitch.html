WorkTape

The recording is the spec.

Problem

Small businesses repeat important work across WhatsApp, spreadsheets, payment notes, and manual follow-ups. They know the workflow, but cannot easily turn it into software requirements.

Most AI app builders still start with a blank prompt. WorkTape starts with observed work.

Solution

WorkTape turns a screen recording of a repetitive workflow into a working internal web app.

The user records the task once. WorkTape extracts frames, analyzes the workflow, asks clarifying questions, creates a structured app spec, and renders a usable internal tool.

MVP

Day 3 demo wedge: WhatsApp/lead management plus spreadsheet tracking -> coaching institute admissions CRM.

The CRM includes intake form, dashboard table, lead statuses, payment/follow-up tracking, WhatsApp message templates, copy actions, and CSV export.

Why Now

Vision models can understand workflow screens. Coding agents can turn structured specs into software. WorkTape combines both into a practical workflow-to-software loop.

Differentiation

WorkTape is not a generic app builder, chatbot, no-code builder, or macro recorder.

The core difference is the input: real screen-recorded work instead of a blank prompt.
